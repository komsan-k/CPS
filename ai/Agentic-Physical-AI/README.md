# Agentic AI vs Physical AI  
## Toward Cyber-Physical Intelligence

## 1. Agentic AI (Decision-Centric Intelligence)
Agentic AI refers to AI systems that behave like autonomous agents capable of perceiving, reasoning, planning, acting, and learning from feedback.

## 2. Physical AI (Embodied Intelligence)
Physical AI refers to AI systems that interact with the real physical world through sensors and actuators.

## 3. Key Differences
- Agentic AI: decision-making
- Physical AI: real-world interaction

## 4. Convergence
Agentic AI + Physical AI = Cyber-Physical Intelligence

## 5. Key Insight
Agentic AI = Brain
Physical AI = Body
