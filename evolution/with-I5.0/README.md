
# 📈 Evolution of Prediction in CPS
## Alignment with Industry 5.0

Prediction in Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) has evolved from reactive correction to human-centric, explainable, and sustainable intelligence. 
This evolution directly supports the principles of Industry 5.0, which emphasizes:

- Human-centricity
- Sustainability
- Resilience
- Ethical AI
- Collaborative intelligence

---

# 🏭 What is Industry 5.0?

Industry 5.0 moves beyond automation efficiency (Industry 4.0) toward:

> Human–machine collaboration  
> Sustainable industrial production  
> Resilient and adaptive systems  

It integrates advanced AI with human values and environmental awareness.

| CPS Generation | Prediction Capability | Industry 5.0 Relevance |

# 🔹 CPS 5.0 as Industry 5.0 Enabler

CPS 5.0 introduces:

- Explainable AI (XAI)
- Human-in-the-loop decision systems
- Ethical governance mechanisms
- Sustainability-aware optimization
- Trust-aware prediction models

### Example: Smart Factory Scenario

Instead of:

Machine will fail in 4 hours.

Industry 5.0-aligned CPS explains:

Bearing degradation rate increased due to overload.  
Predicted failure in 4 hours.  
Recommend load redistribution to reduce environmental impact and avoid overtime labor stress.

# 🌱 Sustainability Alignment

Industry 5.0 emphasizes green transformation.

Predictive CPS supports sustainability by:

- Forecasting energy demand
- Optimizing renewable integration
- Reducing material waste
- Minimizing carbon footprint

Example:

Predict energy peak → Shift production → Reduce grid stress → Lower emissions
